# Don'tThai-to-Leang

A camera-controlled endless runner game powered by computer vision.

**Created by: Nol Chhonleang**

## How to Play
1. **Enable Camera**: The game requires camera access to track your hand.
2. **Pinch Your Hand**: Make a pinch gesture (👌) in the camera view.
3. **Control**: Move your pinched hand **UP** or **DOWN** to control the soldier.
4. **Avoid Bombs**: Dodge incoming obstacles to increase your score.

## Technical Details
*   **Engine**: React + HTML5 Canvas.
*   **Control System**: Custom color-based tracking algorithm (No external AI APIs).
*   **Audio**: Real-time synthesized sound effects (No audio files).
